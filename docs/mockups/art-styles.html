KK Manager: three art styles

Same moment in each: MCH unit, Monday 08:45. The nurse queue is jammed with routine antenatal checks, simple cases are stuck behind them, and the doctor has been idle for 42 minutes. CCMS just went down. These are rough mockups to compare feel, not final art.

Dot above each patient = purpose of visit: Antenatal rutin (perlu jururawat dulu)Kes ringkas (doktor sahaja)PostnatalKesihatan kanak-kanak

1. Top-down 2D my pick

Looking straight down at the floor plan. Characters walk room to room.

Good
  • Easiest to read queues and bottlenecks at a glance
  • Art is quick to make and stays consistent as we add units
  • Light: runs on clinic PCs and older laptops
Trade-off
  • Less "Sims" depth; people are seen from above
  • Character personality comes from bubbles and icons, not faces

2. Isometric 2D

The angled, cut-away "dollhouse" view that feels most like Sims.

Good
  • Most charming and Sims-like; faces and outfits show
  • Feels like a "real" game, great for screenshots in WhatsApp groups
Trade-off
  • Roughly 3x the art work per room and object
  • People overlap; harder to count a queue or click the right person
  • Clicking and layout code are more complex

3. Flat diagram

Rooms as boxes, patients as coloured dots flowing between them.

Good
  • Clearest view of the system: queues, timers, idle time, rules
  • Cheapest to build; feels like the district office's own tools
Trade-off
  • Loses the Sims feel almost completely
  • In-jokes and characters have less room to land

A common middle path: play in style 1 or 2, and use style 3 as the end-of-day report or an "analysis" overlay you can toggle.
